# NAQT Subject Distribution (High School IS Sets)

## Overview
A standard NAQT IS packet contains 24 tossups. The distribution below shows approximate question counts per packet and the sub-topic breakdown within each category.

## History (18.5% — ~4-5 per packet)

### Sub-topics (roughly equal weight post-2020 rebalancing)
- **American History**: Colonial period through present. Presidents, wars, Supreme Court cases, social movements, constitutional amendments.
- **European History**: Ancient Greece/Rome through present. Major wars, revolutions, monarchs, treaties, political movements.
- **World History**: Everything outside Europe and America. Chinese dynasties, Islamic civilizations, African empires, South/Southeast Asian history, Mesoamerican civilizations, Mongol Empire, Ottoman/Mughal/Safavid empires, decolonization.
- **Ancient History**: Pre-500 CE civilizations. Mesopotamia, Egypt, Indus Valley, early China. (Overlaps with the above categories.)

### NAQT History Weighting Notes
- World history has been weighted upward in recent years
- Expect roughly equal representation of American, European, and World
- Ancient history questions often appear as "World" or "European" depending on geography

## Literature (18.5% — ~4-5 per packet)

### Sub-topics
- **American Literature**: Novels, poetry, drama. Twain, Fitzgerald, Morrison, Hemingway, Dickinson, Whitman, Poe, Faulkner, Steinbeck.
- **British Literature**: Shakespeare (heavily tested), Dickens, Austen, Bronte sisters, Milton, Chaucer, Woolf, Orwell.
- **European Literature**: Dostoevsky, Tolstoy, Kafka, Cervantes, Hugo, Dante, Goethe, Camus, Homer.
- **World Literature**: Murasaki Shikibu, Chinua Achebe, Gabriel Garcia Marquez, Rabindranath Tagore, Naguib Mahfouz.
- **Mythology**: Greek/Roman mythology (heavily tested), Norse, Egyptian, Hindu, Mesopotamian, Japanese, Celtic.
- **Bible as Literature**: Major narrative events, characters, and books (treated as literary text, not theology).

### Literature Question Patterns
- "Identify the novel in which [plot event] occurs"
- "Name the author who wrote [work]"
- "In this mythology, [deity/hero] does [action]"
- Character identification, plot events, literary devices

## Science (18.5% — ~4-5 per packet)

### Sub-topics
- **Biology**: Cell biology, genetics, evolution, ecology, anatomy, taxonomy. Darwin, Mendel, DNA/RNA, photosynthesis, ecosystems.
- **Chemistry**: Periodic table, chemical bonds, reactions, acids/bases, organic chemistry, famous chemists. Mendeleev, Lavoisier, Curie.
- **Physics**: Mechanics, thermodynamics, electromagnetism, optics, quantum physics, relativity. Newton, Einstein, Bohr, Faraday.
- **Mathematics**: Number theory, geometry, calculus concepts, famous theorems, famous mathematicians. Euler, Gauss, Fibonacci, Pythagorean theorem.
- **Earth Science**: Geology, meteorology, oceanography, plate tectonics, minerals, rock types.
- **Computer Science**: Algorithms, programming concepts, history of computing. Turing, Babbage, binary.
- **Astronomy**: Planets, stars, galaxies, space exploration, Hubble, Kepler's laws.

### Science Question Patterns
- "Name this element/compound/organism"
- "This law/principle states that..."
- "This scientist discovered/proposed..."
- Process identification (photosynthesis steps, geological processes)

## Current Events (7.5% — ~2 per packet)
- Recent news events (within past 1-2 years)
- Major political developments, elections, international relations
- Supreme Court decisions, legislation
- Natural disasters, major accidents
- Nobel Prize winners, major awards
- NOTE: These questions age rapidly. Build a framework for easy updates.

## Popular Culture (7.5% — ~2 per packet)
- Film, television, music, video games
- Award winners (Oscars, Grammys, Emmys)
- Major franchises and cultural phenomena
- Social media and internet culture
- NOT the same as Fine Arts — this is mainstream entertainment

## Fine Arts (7.0% — ~1-2 per packet)
### Sub-topics
- **Visual Arts**: Painting, sculpture. Renaissance masters, Impressionists, modern art movements. Identify paintings by description.
- **Classical Music**: Composers, major works, musical terms. Bach, Mozart, Beethoven, Tchaikovsky, Stravinsky.
- **Architecture**: Major buildings, architectural styles, architects. Gothic, Baroque, Art Deco, Frank Lloyd Wright.
- **Opera and Theater**: Major operas, playwrights, Broadway.
- **Film as Art**: Directors, landmark films (art cinema, not blockbusters — blockbusters go under Popular Culture).

## Geography (7.0% — ~1-2 per packet)
### Sub-topics
- **Physical Geography**: Mountain ranges, rivers, deserts, oceans, lakes, islands, straits.
- **Political Geography**: Countries, capitals, borders, territories.
- **Cultural Geography**: Languages, religions by region, ethnic groups.
- **Map Skills**: Identifying locations, neighboring countries, relative positions.
- High-frequency: world capitals, major rivers (Nile, Amazon, Yangtze, Danube), mountain ranges (Himalayas, Andes, Alps), straits and canals.

## General Knowledge (5.5% — ~1 per packet)
- Potpourri / miscellaneous
- Word origins, idioms, common knowledge
- Food and cooking
- Technology and inventions
- Holidays and traditions

## Sports (4.0% — ~1 per packet)
- Major professional leagues (NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, soccer)
- Olympics (summer and winter)
- Record holders, Hall of Famers
- Rules and terminology
- College sports (especially football and basketball)

## Social Science (3.5% — ~1 per packet)
### Sub-topics
- **Economics**: Supply/demand, GDP, inflation, Adam Smith, Keynes, market structures
- **Psychology**: Pavlov, Skinner, Freud, Piaget, Milgram experiment, cognitive biases
- **Political Science**: Forms of government, political theorists, international relations concepts
- **Sociology**: Weber, Durkheim, Marx (as sociologist), social structures, demographics
- **Anthropology**: Cultural anthropology, famous anthropologists, human evolution

## Philosophy / Religion (2.0% — ~0-1 per packet)
- Major philosophers: Plato, Aristotle, Kant, Descartes, Nietzsche, Locke, Hume
- World religions: Christianity, Islam, Buddhism, Hinduism, Judaism, Sikhism, Confucianism
- Philosophical concepts: epistemology, ethics, metaphysics, logic
- Religious texts, practices, holy sites

## Foreign Language (0.5% — ~0-1 per packet)
- Common phrases, cognates, translations
- Language families
- Rarely tested at high school level
