# High-Frequency NAQT Topics by Subject

These are the topics that appear most frequently in NAQT question sets. Prioritize these when building question banks. Based on NAQT's "You Gotta Know" articles and community frequency analysis.

## History — High Frequency Topics

### World History
- Chinese dynasties (Shang, Zhou, Qin, Han, Sui, Tang, Song, Yuan, Ming, Qing)
- Mongol Empire (Genghis Khan, Kublai Khan, Golden Horde, Pax Mongolica)
- Islamic caliphates (Umayyad, Abbasid, Ottoman, Safavid, Mughal)
- West African empires (Ghana, Mali, Songhai, Mansa Musa)
- Mesoamerican civilizations (Olmec, Maya, Aztec, Inca)
- Indian empires (Maurya, Gupta, Delhi Sultanate, Mughal)
- Silk Road and Indian Ocean trade
- Columbian Exchange
- Atlantic slave trade
- Decolonization movements (India, Africa, Southeast Asia)
- Russian Revolution and Soviet Union
- Chinese Revolution (Mao, Cultural Revolution, Tiananmen)
- Cold War proxy conflicts

### European History
- Ancient Greece (democracy, philosophy, Persian Wars, Peloponnesian War)
- Roman Republic and Empire (Caesar, Augustus, fall of Rome)
- Crusades
- Black Death
- Renaissance
- Protestant Reformation (Luther, Calvin, Counter-Reformation)
- French Revolution
- Napoleonic Wars
- Industrial Revolution
- German and Italian unification
- World War I (causes, major battles, Treaty of Versailles)
- World War II (Holocaust, D-Day, Pacific Theater, atomic bomb)
- European Union formation

### American History
- Colonial period and American Revolution
- Constitution and Bill of Rights
- Civil War (causes, major battles, Reconstruction)
- Westward expansion and Native American removal
- Progressive Era
- Great Depression and New Deal
- Civil Rights Movement
- Vietnam War and protest movements
- Watergate
- Cold War domestic politics

## Literature — High Frequency Topics

### Novels and Authors (most asked)
- Shakespeare plays: Hamlet, Macbeth, Othello, King Lear, Romeo and Juliet, The Tempest, A Midsummer Night's Dream
- Dostoevsky: Crime and Punishment, The Brothers Karamazov
- Dickens: Great Expectations, A Tale of Two Cities, Oliver Twist
- Twain: Adventures of Huckleberry Finn, Tom Sawyer
- Fitzgerald: The Great Gatsby
- Homer: Iliad, Odyssey
- Austen: Pride and Prejudice, Sense and Sensibility
- Bronte (Charlotte and Emily): Jane Eyre, Wuthering Heights
- Tolstoy: War and Peace, Anna Karenina
- Melville: Moby-Dick
- Hawthorne: The Scarlet Letter
- Cervantes: Don Quixote
- Hugo: Les Miserables
- Kafka: The Metamorphosis, The Trial
- Morrison: Beloved, Song of Solomon
- Garcia Marquez: One Hundred Years of Solitude
- Orwell: 1984, Animal Farm
- Hemingway: The Old Man and the Sea, A Farewell to Arms
- Steinbeck: The Grapes of Wrath, Of Mice and Men
- Faulkner: The Sound and the Fury, As I Lay Dying

### Poetry
- Frost, Dickinson, Whitman, Poe, Keats, Shelley, Byron, Wordsworth, T.S. Eliot, Langston Hughes, Maya Angelou, Yeats, Tennyson

### Mythology (extremely high frequency)
- Greek: Zeus, Hera, Athena, Apollo, Hermes, Poseidon, Hades, Ares, Aphrodite
- Greek heroes: Heracles, Perseus, Theseus, Odysseus, Achilles, Jason
- Greek myths: Trojan War, Twelve Labors, Prometheus, Pandora, Orpheus, Icarus
- Roman equivalents of Greek gods
- Norse: Odin, Thor, Loki, Freya, Ragnarok, Yggdrasil
- Egyptian: Ra, Osiris, Isis, Anubis, Horus, Set
- Hindu: Vishnu, Shiva, Brahma, Ganesh, Rama, Krishna

## Science — High Frequency Topics

### Biology
- Cell structure (organelles, mitosis, meiosis)
- DNA/RNA (replication, transcription, translation)
- Mendelian genetics (dominant/recessive, Punnett squares)
- Evolution (natural selection, speciation, Darwin)
- Taxonomy (kingdom, phylum, etc.)
- Photosynthesis and cellular respiration
- Ecology (food webs, biomes, symbiosis)
- Human body systems (circulatory, nervous, digestive)

### Chemistry
- Periodic table (groups, periods, element properties)
- Chemical bonding (ionic, covalent, metallic)
- Acids and bases (pH scale)
- Types of reactions (synthesis, decomposition, combustion)
- Gas laws (Boyle's, Charles's, ideal gas)
- Atomic structure (electrons, protons, neutrons, orbitals)
- Organic chemistry basics (hydrocarbons, functional groups)
- Famous chemists (Mendeleev, Lavoisier, Dalton, Curie)

### Physics
- Newton's laws of motion
- Thermodynamics (laws, entropy, heat transfer)
- Electromagnetism (Faraday, Maxwell, circuits)
- Optics (reflection, refraction, lenses)
- Wave properties (frequency, wavelength, interference)
- Einstein's relativity (special and general)
- Quantum mechanics basics (Bohr model, uncertainty principle)
- Simple machines and mechanical advantage

### Math
- Pythagorean theorem
- Pi and circle properties
- Prime numbers, Fibonacci sequence
- Basic calculus concepts (derivatives, integrals)
- Famous theorems (Fermat's Last, Euler's identity)
- Famous mathematicians (Euler, Gauss, Fibonacci, Archimedes, Newton, Leibniz)

## Geography — High Frequency Topics

### Physical
- World's longest rivers (Nile, Amazon, Yangtze, Mississippi, Danube, Mekong)
- Major mountain ranges (Himalayas, Andes, Alps, Rockies, Urals, Atlas)
- Deserts (Sahara, Gobi, Arabian, Atacama, Kalahari)
- Oceans and seas
- Major straits and canals (Suez, Panama, Strait of Hormuz, Strait of Malacca, Bosporus)
- Major islands (Greenland, Borneo, Madagascar, New Guinea)

### Political
- World capitals (especially non-obvious ones: Ankara not Istanbul, Canberra not Sydney)
- Countries by continent
- Landlocked countries
- Countries that have changed names (e.g., Burma/Myanmar, Persia/Iran)
- Territorial disputes

## Fine Arts — High Frequency Topics

### Visual Arts
- Leonardo da Vinci: Mona Lisa, The Last Supper, Vitruvian Man
- Michelangelo: David, Sistine Chapel ceiling, Pieta
- Rembrandt: The Night Watch, self-portraits
- Van Gogh: Starry Night, Sunflowers
- Monet: Water Lilies, Impression Sunrise
- Picasso: Guernica, Les Demoiselles d'Avignon
- Dali: The Persistence of Memory
- Art movements: Renaissance, Baroque, Impressionism, Cubism, Surrealism, Abstract Expressionism

### Classical Music
- Bach, Mozart, Beethoven, Tchaikovsky, Brahms, Handel, Vivaldi
- Beethoven's symphonies (especially 5th, 9th)
- Mozart's operas (Magic Flute, Marriage of Figaro, Don Giovanni)
- Tchaikovsky's ballets (Swan Lake, Nutcracker, Sleeping Beauty)
- Musical terms: allegro, forte, crescendo, sonata, symphony, concerto

## Social Science — High Frequency Topics

### Economics
- Supply and demand, market equilibrium
- GDP, inflation, unemployment
- Adam Smith (The Wealth of Nations, invisible hand)
- Keynes (Keynesian economics, deficit spending)
- Marx (Das Kapital, labor theory of value)
- Federal Reserve, monetary policy
- Fiscal policy, trade deficits

### Psychology
- Pavlov (classical conditioning)
- Skinner (operant conditioning)
- Freud (psychoanalysis, id/ego/superego)
- Milgram experiment (obedience to authority)
- Stanford prison experiment (Zimbardo)
- Maslow's hierarchy of needs
- Piaget (cognitive development stages)
- Cognitive biases (confirmation bias, anchoring)

## Philosophy — High Frequency Topics
- Plato (Republic, Allegory of the Cave, Theory of Forms)
- Aristotle (Nicomachean Ethics, logic, categories)
- Descartes (Cogito ergo sum, Meditations, mind-body dualism)
- Kant (Critique of Pure Reason, categorical imperative)
- Nietzsche (Thus Spoke Zarathustra, Ubermensch, will to power)
- Locke (social contract, tabula rasa, natural rights)
- Hobbes (Leviathan, state of nature)
- Utilitarian thinkers (Bentham, Mill)
